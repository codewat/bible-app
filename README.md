# ✝️ Catholic Holy Bible & Prayer Book (73 Books)

A modern, responsive, 100% offline Progressive Web App (PWA) for the Catholic Holy Bible, featuring the complete 73-book Catholic Canon (Douay-Rheims version with all Deuterocanonical books), the Holy Rosary with an interactive bead tracker, the Divine Mercy Chaplet, Traditional Catholic Prayers, emotional guidance ("How are you feeling today?"), multi-verse combine & bookmarking, and offline Audio Bible.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 📱 100% Offline Progressive Web App (PWA)
- **Installable on Phone & Mac/PC**: Install as a standalone native app via Safari ("Add to Home Screen") or Chrome/Edge/Brave ("Install App").
- **Offline Service Worker**: Automatically caches scriptures, prayers, and assets so the entire app works seamlessly even without internet.
- **Custom Catholic Gold Cross Icon & Manifest**: Includes standalone window mode and immersive experience without browser URL bars.

### 2. 📿 Interactive Catholic Prayers & Holy Rosary
- **The Most Holy Rosary**:
  - Automatically identifies and highlights **today's Mystery** (Joyful, Luminous, Sorrowful, Glorious).
  - Meditations, scripture citations, and spiritual fruits for all 20 decades.
  - **Interactive Bead Tracker**: Tap through the Our Father bead, 10 Hail Mary beads, and the Glory Be / Fatima Prayer beads with visual feedback!
- **The Chaplet of the Divine Mercy**:
  - Complete 3 o'clock opening prayer, Eternal Father prayers, Sorrowful Passion decade responses, and concluding Doxology.
- **Daily Traditional Catholic Prayers**:
  - *The Angelus* & *Regina Caeli*
  - *Saint Michael the Archangel Prayer*
  - *The Memorare of St. Bernard*
  - *Anima Christi (Soul of Christ)*
  - *Act of Contrition*
  - *Daily Morning Offering*
  - *Night Prayer & Examination of Conscience*
  - *Guardian Angel Prayer (Angele Dei)*
- **Audio Support**: Listen to any prayer or decade spoken aloud with peaceful voice synthesis.

### 3. ✨ "How are you feeling today?" Welcome & Emotional Guidance
- Welcomes you upon opening with 10+ emotional states (Joyful, Sad, Anxious, Tired, Afraid, Lonely, Confused, Angry, Needing Mercy, Tempted, Sick).
- Connects your current feeling to Catholic scripture and a Holy Reflection.
- Skip directly to the reader or re-open anytime from the top bar.

### 4. 📖 Multi-Verse Selection & Custom Bookmark Pop-up
- **Combine Multiple Verses**: Click any verse, then click subsequent verses to combine them (e.g. *Matthew 5:3–5*).
- **Floating Action Dock**: Appears smoothly on the side with quick actions:
  - 🔖 *Save to Bookmark*
  - 🔊 *Listen (Audio)*
  - 📋 *Copy with Citation*
  - ✕ *Cancel*
- **Save Bookmark Features**:
  - 🎨 *Highlight Color*: Yellow, Green, Blue, Pink, Purple, or No Color.
  - 💖 *"How does this verse make you feel?"*: Tag with feeling chips (Peaceful, Inspired, Comforted, Grateful, Loved by God, Convicted, Hopeful, Strengthened, Healed, Guided).
  - ✍️ *Personal Reflection / Note*: Write personal meditations or prayer notes that stay attached to the verse.

### 5. 🎨 5 Reading Themes & Typography Customization
- **5 Themes**:
  - 🏛️ **Ivory Light**: Classic warm parchment paper.
  - 📜 **Warm Sepia**: Evening reading with soft leather/coffee tones.
  - 🌌 **Marian Celestial Blue**: Deep mystical Catholic navy blue with gold accents.
  - 🌑 **Midnight Dark**: Pure dark mode for battery saving and night reading.
  - 🌿 **Gethsemane Olive**: Contemplative dark olive green.
- **Typography**:
  - *Holy Serif* (Classic Georgia / Cambria)
  - *Clean Sans* (Modern System Sans)
  - *Book Antiqua* (Palatino / Literary Antiqua)
- **4 Font Sizes**: Small, Default, Large, Extra Large.

### 6. 🔊 100% Offline Audio Bible
- Speeds: 0.8x, 1.0x, 1.2x, 1.5x.
- Real-time verse-by-verse highlighting as it reads aloud.
- Play whole chapters or selected verses.
